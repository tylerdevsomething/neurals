The lighthouse keeper kept a notebook on the table by the window. Every evening she wrote the time the lamp was lit, the speed of the wind, and the names of the ships that passed. On calm nights the sea was flat and grey, and the light swept across it like a slow hand. On stormy nights the waves climbed the rocks and the glass rattled in its frame.

In the spring a small boat arrived with flour, oil, letters and a box of new batteries. The captain stayed for tea. He said the harbour town had built a school, a bakery and a bridge over the river. She asked whether the old bridge was still standing. He said it was, but nobody used it any more.

def average(values):
    if not values:
        return 0.0
    total = 0.0
    for v in values:
        total += v
    return total / len(values)

print(average([3, 4, 5, 6]))
